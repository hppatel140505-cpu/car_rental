Our Terms & Chauffeur Policies

Clear, simple, and fair guidelines designed for a premium chauffeur-driven travel experience.

Travel with Complete Peace of Mind

At DriveEase, we believe in 100% transparency. No driving worries, no hidden charges. Please review our official chauffeured rental policies below before booking your ride.

🚀

Kilometre Limit Policy

Perfect for long-distance outstation travel and intercity road trips, we offer extremely generous daily mileage:

  • 1,000 Kilometres Included: Every 24-hour booking cycle includes 1,000 km of complimentary travel.
  • Extra Mileage Charge: Driving beyond the daily 1,000 km limit will attract an excess rate of ₹15.00 per additional kilometre.
⛽

Fuel Policy

Simple and transparent fuel policies based on actual consumption during your outstation journey:

  • Start Full: The vehicle is dispatched with a full tank of fuel.
  • Pay Actuals: Refueling costs during and at the end of the trip are billed to you directly at actual petrol/diesel pump prices.
💳

Zero Security Deposit

Since all vehicles are driven exclusively by our highly trained professional chauffeurs:

  • ₹0 Deposit Required: No security deposit hold is required from you. Book with complete financial freedom.
  • Zero Damage Liability: Since you are not driving, you have absolutely zero liability for any road mishaps or vehicle damage.
🤵

Professional Chauffeurs

We employ only certified, highly experienced drivers to ensure maximum safety and comfort:

  • No License Needed: You do not need to present a driving license or have driving experience to book.
  • Verified Experts: All chauffeurs are fully background-checked, route-expert, and highly trained in premium customer hospitality.
⏳

Extension & Driver Allowances

We offer flexible extensions for long journeys and cover basic driver halted night rests:

  • Easy Trip Extensions: Extend your travel mid-way by calling our support. Surcharge of basic daily rental rate applies.
  • Driver Night Halt: For outstation trips running past 10:00 PM, a standard outstation night allowance of ₹500 per night is added.
🗺️

Tolls & Interstate Travel

Our entire chauffeured fleet comes with the flexibility to travel freely across national and state borders:

  • All India Tourist Permits: All cars carry valid commercial transport tourist permits for hassle-free border crossings.
  • Tolls & Parking: State border taxes, highway tolls (Fastag), and parking fees incurred are to be paid directly by the customer.

Planning a Long Outstation Road Trip?

Our concierge team is available 24/7 to help design custom itineraries, calculate estimated tolls, or coordinate driver pairings.
